Window Control Icons

You asked for the Bold Duotone circle icons on the window controls: Minimize, Maximize and Close. Each card shows a real Orion title bar with the real window controls as they appear in a frameless Windows window, at rest, with each button hovered, and maximized (Maximize becomes Restore), in light and in dark.

Hover any button in a preview to try it for real. Tapping the previews never answers; tap a card's header to pick it.

1. Which Icons

Line duotone options C and D are new. Only the glyphs differ: the fills, sizes, hover layers and the red Close are the shipped ones. Restore for A is the suggested quit-full-screen-circle.
