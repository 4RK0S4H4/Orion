Calm Dialogs and Docked Glass

You said dialogs felt in your face and weird on a phone, and the docked bars looked odd with the edge glow. Each card below is the real Showcase in a phone frame: A is how it was, B is the new Orion. Swipe a row sideways on a phone.

  • Fewer dialogs. Only questions the app cannot go on without stay dialogs (delete for good, leave unsaved work, a permission). Forms moved to panels or in place; filters became a tool panel with no Apply.
  • On a phone a dialog sits in the bottom of the screen, in reach of your thumb, with its answers side by side and the destructive one quieter. The scrim is half as dark in light mode, and the card is firmer glass, so the page behind is not noise.
  • Swipe it down to put it away, or tap outside. Try it in the B frames or on the Showcase and the dialog page.
  • Docked bars (nav bar, top bar once you scroll, docked toolbar) keep the glass but lose the rim and glow: one hairline on the edge facing the content, like the iPhone tab bar. Menus, buttons, panels and dialogs that float keep their edge.
