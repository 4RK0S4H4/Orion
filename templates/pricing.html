Billing Questions

Ask in the forum, or email billing and an engineer, not a bot, answers within a day.

What happens if I go over on Hobby?
New jobs wait in the queue until the month resets or you upgrade. Nothing is dropped and we never charge a card you haven’t added.
Can I set a spending limit?
Yes, on every paid plan. Choose a monthly cap; at 100% Spool pauses new jobs, or keeps going and emails you, whichever you pick.
Do yearly plans include more jobs?
The same jobs each month, for two months less. Extra jobs are billed monthly at the same rate.
Is my data stored with you?
Job payloads are encrypted and deleted when the run history expires. On Scale, jobs and payloads stay in your own cloud account.
