How We Work

Slowly at the Start, So It Goes Fast on Site

  1. Listen

    We spend the first month with the people who will use the building: teachers at break, swimmers at six in the morning.

  2. Draw

    Plans by hand first, then a model everyone can hold. Nothing is rendered until the plan is right.

  3. Build

    An architect from the team is on site every week, and the contractor has our phone numbers.

  4. Return

    We go back after one year and after five, measure what we got wrong, and publish it.
