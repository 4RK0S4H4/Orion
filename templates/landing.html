The Sensor

Small Enough to Forget. Clever Enough That You Can.

  1. Light Sensor

    Under the frosted cap. Tells you if the spot you chose is too dark, measured in real hours of useful light.

  2. One Small Light

    Blinks once, slowly, when the plant needs you. No beeps, no glow at night.

  3. Moisture Probe

    Capacitive, sealed in glass-filled nylon, so it never rusts and never leaches anything into the soil.

  4. Root Thermometer

    At the tip, where the roots are. Warns you before a cold windowsill does damage.

How It Works

Push It In. Pair It. Stop Guessing.

Setup takes a minute per plant. After a week Sprig knows how fast your pot dries out in your light, and its advice gets specific.

  1. Push It Into the Soil

    Halfway between the stem and the rim, down to the line. Any pot from 8 cm wide.

  2. It Learns Your Plant

    Pick the plant in the app, or scan a leaf. Sprig watches how quickly the pot dries in your light and your season.

Visit Help
Does it work in any soil?
Potting mix, cactus mix, bark, coir and LECA. For semi-hydro, switch the plant to Reservoir in the app and Sprig reads the water line instead.
How far from my phone can it be?
About 10 metres over Bluetooth. With a Thread border router at home, anywhere in the house, and you get nudges when you’re out.

Batch two ships in the first half of March 2027, in order of reservation. You pay nothing until it leaves our warehouse in Utrecht.
