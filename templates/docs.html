Connect a Bank

Ledgerly pays bills from the bank account you choose. Connecting takes under a minute and gives us read-only access until you approve a payment.

Before You Start

You need the online banking sign-in for the account you pay bills from. Ledgerly never sees or stores that password: you type it into your bank’s own page.

  • Over 12,000 banks and credit unions in the US, the UK and the EU are supported.
  • Business accounts work the same way as personal ones.
  • You can connect one bank on Free, five on Plus and as many as you like on Team.

Connect in Four Steps

  1. Open Settings and choose Banks.
  2. Choose Add a Bank and search for yours by name.
  3. Sign in on your bank’s page and allow read-only access.
  4. Pick the account bills should come from. Done.
A payment always needs your tap, or an autopay rule you set. Read-only access lets us see balances and due dates; it cannot move money.

Connect With the API

Teams can start a connection from their own tools. Create a key in Settings › Developers, then ask for a connection link:

curl https://api.ledgerly.example/v2/bank-links \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LEDGERLY_KEY" \
  -d country=GB \
  -d return_url=https://your.app/banks/done

The response holds a url to send the person to. When they finish, we call your return_url with the new account’s id.

What We Store

The account name, the last four digits, the balance and the transactions that match a bill. We keep them for as long as the bank stays connected, and delete them 30 days after you disconnect.

Disconnect a Bank

In Settings › Banks, choose the bank and then Disconnect. Bills on autopay from that account pause and ask you for another one. You can undo for ten seconds.
