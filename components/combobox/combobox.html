Components / Combobox

A text field that suggests as you type. The list opens in glass right under the field (above it when there is no room, as over a phone’s keyboard), the part that matches is bold, and the cursor never leaves the field: arrows move through the options, Enter chooses, Escape closes. One value; the trailing button shows every option while the field is empty and clears it once there is text.

Fixed Options

Options come from a <template> inside the field, with groups and a line of supporting text. Without data-free only an option may be chosen; anything else is an error when you leave the field.

Where the account is registered.
A value chosen earlier is marked in the list.

Free Text

data-free: the options are suggestions; whatever is typed is the value.

Pick a suggestion or write your own.

Search As You Type

S4.combobox.source(field, fn) fetches results for what was typed (here a pretend server that takes a moment). The list shows Searching…, then the results, “No matches” or an error with Try Again. Type “zz” for no matches.

Search by name or team.
This one is offline: type anything to see the error.

In a Form

Inside .s4-form the combobox is checked like any field: when you leave it, never while you type. Errors keep the field’s normal fill.

Compact and in a Floating Panel

In compact density the field and its options are pointer-sized. In a floating panel the field is a tint of the glass, like every field there.
