Title Bars

The default centers the title. data-variant="tabs" makes the document tabs the title bar; data-variant="unified" is a taller bar that holds the window's toolbar. data-controls="start" keeps room for a Mac's traffic lights (drawn here only as a picture of where they go). Everything you click stays clickable; the rest drags the window.

Window Controls

For a frameless window on Windows or Linux whose shell draws no buttons of its own. They act like Windows' own: each is a full cell, the bar's whole height and 46 wide, with no gap to the window's top and end edges, so throwing the pointer into the top corner hits Close and the top edge hits the control below it. What is drawn stays Orion: a firm filled layer on hover and press, Orion's duotone icons, Close turning error only on hover and press. Maximized, Maximize becomes Restore. Right to left, they mirror to the start, as Windows mirrors such a window. On a Mac the system draws the traffic lights; on an installed web app the window controls overlay does, and these step out.

Inspector

A resizable pane at the window's end for the properties of what is selected: drag its start edge, or focus the edge and use the arrow keys. Groups fold. From 840 wide; on a phone the same properties belong in the item's own page.
