Components / Board

Lanes of cards that move from stage to stage: stories, tickets, a sales pipeline, a week. Each lane counts its cards (with an optional limit), takes a new card in place at its foot, and a card moves by dragging, with a finger (hold, then drag) or with the keyboard: Alt and the arrow keys. On a phone the lanes snap one by one. Full screen: Pressroom.

A Board With A Limit

Editing has a limit of two: past it the count turns the error tone, and reads "3 / 2". Add a card at a lane's foot; the app makes it from the s4-lane-add event.
