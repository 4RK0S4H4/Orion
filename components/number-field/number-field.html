Components / Number Field

A number with minus and plus in one box. Tap to step, hold to keep stepping; arrows, Page Up and Page Down from the keyboard; or just type. The value sits in the middle in tabular figures with its unit, and it is read and shown in the page’s language.

Steppers

data-min, data-max, data-step on the input; a unit as the field’s prefix or suffix. Minus stops at the minimum, plus at the maximum.
