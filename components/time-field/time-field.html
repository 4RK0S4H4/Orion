Components / Time Field

Type a time any way you like (9, 930, 9:30 pm, 21.30) or pick one from a short list. Times are shown in the page’s language, with 12 or 24 hours, and stored as HH:MM. No clock dial: typing and a short list are quicker on every device.

Times

data-step sets the list’s spacing; data-min and data-max its ends. The hours follow the page’s language unless data-hour-cycle says 12 or 24.

Try typing “545p”.
Between 8 AM and 6 PM.
Runs every night.

Date and Time, Time Range

A time pairs with the date picker in .s4-date-time. For a range, the end field names its start with data-range-start: its list begins after the start and says how long each choice is.

Error, Disabled and Compact

A time that can’t be read, or one outside the limits, is an error when you leave the field.
