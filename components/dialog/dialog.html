Components / Dialog

A dialog asks a question the app cannot go on without: something serious that cannot be undone, leaving with unsaved work, or a permission. Everything else goes somewhere calmer first (decision 0047): a reply or a rename in place, choices in a menu, a short task in a floating panel, reading on a page. On a wide window it is a modest card in the middle; on a phone it sits in the bottom of the screen within reach of your thumb, its answers side by side, and a tap outside, a swipe down, Escape or Back puts it away (decision 0064).

The Three Questions

Destructive and final, unsaved work, and a permission. The dialog itself takes focus, never a field and never the destructive answer.

Not a Dialog

Rename edits the name where it is; archive just happens and offers Undo; a bill's details open as a page. None of them stop the app to ask.

Delete this bill?

Staples, $72.40, due Nov 2. It goes from every device and can’t be undone.

Close without saving?

Your note to Comcast Business hasn’t been saved.

Remind you before bills are due?

A notification two days before each due date. You can change this in Settings.
