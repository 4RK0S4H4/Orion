Components / Forms

Forms that behave: an error appears when you leave a field, never while you are still typing; fixing it shows a quiet check; settings save by themselves; and nothing asks “Are you sure?” when Undo will do.

A Form You Send

Type half an email address and move on: the message appears. Go back and finish it: the message goes as soon as it is right. Send with something missing and every problem is shown at once.

We only use it for receipts.

Settings That Save Themselves

No Save button. Change something: it reads “Saving…” and then “Saved”.

Undo Instead Of “Are You Sure?”

The action happens at once and the snackbar offers the way back. Keep a confirm for what is serious and cannot be undone.
