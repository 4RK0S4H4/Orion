Components / Motion

Every transition in Orion is one of ten named patterns. Nothing else is allowed: npm run check rejects it. Use the bar at the bottom to freeze any of them and drag through it.

The Philosophy

  1. Motion says what happened, nothing more. A thing moves because it changed, arrived, left or went somewhere.
  2. Subtle but liquid. Quick, never instant. The interesting moments belong to ink: the solid fills that show state.
  3. Glass moves plainly. A few pixels and a fade. It never stretches, scales or slides in from a screen edge.
  4. What you touched answers where you touched it. Controls change in place; new content opens where you asked for it.
  5. One pattern per situation, the same everywhere. A new component picks a pattern; it never invents one.
  6. Sturdy. One transition per element. Nothing cloned. Under reduced motion only fades remain.
