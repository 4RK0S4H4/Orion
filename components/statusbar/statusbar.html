Components / Status Bar

A slim solid bar along the bottom of a desktop window for the state of things: is it saved and synced, what is selected, where the cursor is, the zoom, the connection. Quiet chrome: small type, no fills at rest. A segment you can click is a button that opens a menu or a panel, and what it opens is always reachable another way too. In a window it shows from 840 wide.

An Editor's Status

Sync and the cursor's line on the left; the language, the zoom and the connection on the right. The zoom opens a menu, the language runs a command. Select some text to see the count change (it is announced politely).
