Components / Direct Manipulation

Move things with your hand instead of through a menu: drag to reorder, swipe a row for its actions, select several rows and act on all of them. Everything here also works with the keyboard.

Drag To Reorder

Drag a row by its grip, with a mouse or a finger. The other rows make room and the row settles where you drop it. Keyboard: focus a grip, press Space, move with the arrow keys, press Space again.

Between Lists

Lists that share a group pass items to each other. With no grip the whole card drags: at once with a mouse, after a short hold with a finger.

Swipe A Row

On a touch screen, drag a row to the left to uncover its actions. A long swipe archives. With a keyboard, Tab reaches the same buttons.

Select Several

Hold a row (or Ctrl-click it, or tap its picture) to start selecting; then tap rows to add or remove them. The bar shows how many and what you can do with them. Escape ends it.
