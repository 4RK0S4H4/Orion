Components / Charts

A small set of chart styles drawn from the tokens. Hover or tap a chart for its values, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Every chart has a Table button that shows the same numbers as rows. Try the Color control above: a chart with one series wears the accent.

One Series

Columns for amounts over time, a line or an area for a trend, bars for ranking things with long names.

Several Series

Up to six, in a fixed order of colors that stay apart for color-blind readers. A legend names them; the text itself is never colored.

Time Range and Series

A range control above the chart: the ranges are a button group (they change how the same chart is shown), Custom opens a date range in place. With data-legend="toggle" each name in the legend is a button that shows or hides its series (one always stays); the Table keeps every series.

Parts Of A Whole

A ring, for five parts at most. The total sits in the middle and the parts are named beside it.

Spark

A small line beside a number. No axes: the number says the value, the line says which way it is going.
