Components / Button Group

Button groups put related buttons together. A standard group spaces buttons 8dp apart and reacts as one: press and hold a button to see it widen while its neighbours give way. A connected group is the Ink Track (decision 0059): one filled track with the options on it, and an accent ink pill that slides to your choice, its front edge leading and its back edge trailing.

Button group or tabs? If it swaps what is on the screen, it is tabs. If it changes a value or how the same thing is shown, it is a button group. If it narrows a collection and there can be many, it is chips.

Standard

Actions for the current bill. Press and hold.

Connected, Single Select

data-connected data-select="single": exactly one option is selected; the ink slides to it. The period of one chart, the status a list is filtered by: the same content, shown another way. Tab to the group, then use the arrow keys and Space. Stretches to fill its width by default.

Showing: month

A pair is a group with two options, drawn the same way.

Connected, Multi Select and Fit Content

data-select="multi" toggles each option: each selected option fills in place, and selected neighbours fuse into one pill. data-fit="content" keeps the group as wide as its labels. Icon options need an aria-label.

Sizes

The group follows its buttons' data-size; the track's corner is always the option's corner plus its padding (concentric).

On Glass

Inside a glass bar or panel the glass is the track and the ink is a tint of the accent (items on glass are tints); every label is on-surface.

Any Accent, Firm Corners

The ink is the accent; the track and the ink follow the corner dial (here S4.theme({ seed: 'teal', corners: 'firm', surfaces: 'tinted' }, board)).

Disabled Option

A disabled button is skipped by the arrow keys.
