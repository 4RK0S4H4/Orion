Media

.s4-media is a figure; .s4-media-box holds an img, video or iframe at a fixed ratio (data-ratio: 16:9 default, 4:3, 3:2, 1:1, 4:5, 21:9), so nothing jumps while it loads. Without media it shows its empty state; a play control floats over a poster. Tones: default, low, secondary, tertiary, accent.
