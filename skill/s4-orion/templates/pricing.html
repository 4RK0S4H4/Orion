Billing Questions

Anything else, write to us and a person answers within a day.

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrades start at once and we credit what’s left of your current plan; downgrades start at your next renewal.
What happens after the free trial?
You choose a plan or go back to Free. We never charge without asking, and your bills stay where they are.
Do you offer refunds?
Within 30 days of any payment, no questions asked.
