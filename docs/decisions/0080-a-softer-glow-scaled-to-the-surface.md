# 0080: A softer glow, scaled to the surface (2026-11-25)

**Status:** the owner's call (issue 106, review lab [`explore/review-glass-glow.html`](../../explore/review-glass-glow.html), answers: strength B "60%", depth B "Scaled To Height", combined A "Softer"). Amends [0034](0034-ios-calibrated-edge.md), [0036](0036-dynamic-edge-glow-and-shade.md) and [0037](0037-glow-shade-flip.md): the glow's strength and how far it reaches. The rim, the flip at OKLCH lightness 0.6, the same geometry in light and dark, the edge widths of 0029 and the docked hairline of 0064 stand.

The owner found the glow inside the glass edge too strong on short surfaces: it reached a fixed 12px in from the top and the bottom at every size, so a 24px tooltip or a 52px snackbar was mostly glow, while a tall panel barely showed it. The lab kept today's glass and rim and changed only the glow.

**What we do now** (`components/glass/`, `tokens/src/glass.tokens.json`):
- **Strength 60%.** `--s4-glass-glow-alpha` (the white glow on dark glass) is 0.144, `--s4-glass-glow-shade-alpha` (the ink shade on light glass) 0.168: 60% of 0.24 and 0.28. The flip stays at L 0.6.
- **Depth 15% of the height, 3 to 12px.** The glow or shade reaches `--s4-glass-glow-ratio` (0.15, new) of the surface's height, never less than a quarter of `--s4-glass-glow-size` (3px) or more than the size itself (12px, unchanged). On the Showcase that is 3.6px on a tooltip, 7.2 on a snackbar, 8.4 on a FAB and an extended FAB, 9.6 on the glass toolbar, 12 on menus, dialogs and panels; at 80px and up a surface gets the full 12.
- **No script, no layout.** `box-shadow` takes no percentages and container query units would need `container-type`, which changes how a surface is sized (a `size` container loses its content height, an `inline-size` one still gives no height unit). So each short component names its least height in a private `--_glass-h` from the same size tokens that size it: the tooltip from `space-1` and the body-small line height, the snackbar and a one-item menu from the touch target, the toolbar from `size-toolbar`, the FAB from its own `--_h` (56, 80, 96), the composer from the touch target; a vertical toolbar of two tools, a menu of two items and a rich tooltip with actions are tall. Density, the scale dial and text size change those tokens, so the depth follows them on the first paint. `--_glass-h` is registered as not inherited, so a surface inside another never takes its parent's height; anything that names none (dialogs, panels, the site's phone panel) gets the full 12px. The value is a lower bound: a snackbar that wraps or a toolbar at 200% text grows and its glow only gets relatively smaller.
- **A small measure for the app's own glass.** `glass/glass.js` (core pack and full bundle, not the site bundle, whose only glass is the tall phone panel) watches each `.s4-glass` element with one ResizeObserver and writes its layout height into `--_glass-h`. It reads `offsetHeight`, so a pop or a rise (transforms) never changes it, a hidden element keeps its last value, and nothing is animated; with nothing measured it keeps the full depth. It changes no layout, so nothing re-wraps mid-motion (0072).
- **Guarantees kept.** Docked bars draw the hairline only (0064); high contrast, reduced transparency, `data-glass="off"` and browsers without backdrop-filter are solid; `check-glass` is unchanged (the glow only gets weaker). FABs now share the glass rim and glow rules instead of a copy of them, which keeps the site bundle inside its 150 KB.
- **Proof.** `glowAudit()` (`skill/orion/scripts/silent.mjs`) reads each glass surface's depth from its computed `box-shadow` and fails one that reaches more than 15% of its height plus 1px: in the page checker at rest, and in the suite at load, with every menu, dialog and panel open, with a tooltip shown and with a snackbar up. `tests/fixtures/bad-glow.html` (an app that turned the ratio up to 0.4) fails it (`npm run test:checker`).
