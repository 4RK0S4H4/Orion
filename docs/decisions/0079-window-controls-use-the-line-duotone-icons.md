# 0079: Window controls use the line duotone icons (2026-11-24)

**Status:** the owner's call (issue 105, review lab [`explore/review-window-icons.html`](../../explore/review-window-icons.html), answer "C, Line duotone (proposed)"). Amends [0074](0074-window-controls-use-the-duotone-icons.md): the glyphs and the second tone. Cells, corner rule, firm layer and the rest of 0072, 0074 and 0077 stand.

The owner asked for the Bold Duotone circle icons on the window controls. The lab showed four sets (round, square as shipped, and two Line Duotone sets); he chose the plain Line Duotone marks without a tile or circle.

**What we do now** (`components/desktop/`, `components/icons/`):
- **Four names, new bodies.** In the Solar set `window-minimize`, `window-maximize`, `window-restore` and `window-close` are `minus-line-duotone`, `full-screen-line-duotone`, `quit-full-screen-line-duotone` and `close-line-duotone` (Solar, CC BY 4.0, vendored in `components/icons/solar.json`, already credited). The marks are strokes (`stroke="currentColor"`, round caps, 1.5 at 24, fill none) with half of each mark drawn in a second tone; the page's `.s4-icon > svg [opacity]` rule already tones it, so no new icon code was needed. Forced colors still draw them in `ButtonText` and `HighlightText`.
- **Phosphor is unchanged.** Orion's Phosphor sets are regular and bold duotone (filled tile plus a mark); they have no stroke-only equivalent (no duotone `x`, and `minus` is a tile too). Keeping `minus-square`, `corners-out`, `corners-in` and `x-square` there means one family per set; a Phosphor app keeps the square tiles of 0074.
- **One second tone, 0.65.** `--s4-window-control-tone` (token `s4.window-control-tone`, 0.65) is the second tone's opacity at rest, on hover and on press, for every control. It replaces 0074's 0.5 on Close's error fill: with no tile to separate from the red, the two halves sit close and read as one mark.
- **Contrast** (measured on the built page, stroke colour against the pixels behind it, tone composited at 0.65): the first tone (the full stroke) against the bar is 16.4:1 light, 15.2:1 dark, 18.6:1 and 16.5:1 in high contrast; against Close's error fill it is 6.5:1 light, 7.5:1 dark, 14.0:1 and 18.4:1 in high contrast. The second tone at 0.65 is 5.4:1 (light), 7.0:1 (dark), 6.7:1 and 7.7:1 (high contrast) on the bar and 3.4:1, 3.8:1, 6.3:1 and 6.7:1 on Close's fill, so both tones are 3:1 or more everywhere (0074's tile and mark could not both reach it). The two tones sit 1.9 to 3.1:1 apart on purpose: one mark, not a tile and a symbol.
- **Proof.** The suite's stubbed Windows shell still requires each control to draw its `window-*` icon at 16px or more and Maximize to draw `window-restore` while maximized.
